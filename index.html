Hold'em Coach

Learn No-Limit Texas Hold'em, one decision at a time.

Hold'em Coach teaches one solid poker system with short lessons, drills on real hands, a table to play against computer players, and cheat sheets. It runs offline on iPhone and iPad.

Support

Found a problem or have a question? Open an issue and we will answer there.

Common questions

Where is my progress saved? On your device only. Deleting the app deletes it. Settings → Reset progress starts again at level 1.

Is there real money? No. Hold'em Coach is for learning: there is no real-money gambling, no chips to buy and no prizes.

Do I need an account? No. There is no account and no sign-in.
