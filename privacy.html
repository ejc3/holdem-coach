Privacy policy

Hold'em Coach, by Dolphin Labs. Effective November 12, 2026.

The short version

Hold'em Coach does not collect any data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not send anything over the internet.

What stays on your device

Your progress (levels, streak, XP and which concepts are due for review) and your settings are saved in the app's own storage on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. If your device backs up apps with iCloud or a computer, that backup is between you and Apple, under Apple's privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Settings → Reset progress erases your progress. Deleting the app erases everything it saved.

Children

Hold'em Coach is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
